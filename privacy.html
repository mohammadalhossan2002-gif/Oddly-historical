Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy covers the Oddly Historical Uploader, a private application operated by the owner of the YouTube channel Oddly Historical (@OddlyHistoricalStories). The application is used only by the channel owner to manage their own channel.

YouTube API Services

The application uses the YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data is accessed

  • Upload of the channel owner's own videos, titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails.
  • Management of the channel owner's own videos and playlists.
  • Read-only access to the channel owner's own YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time, audience retention).
  • Public data of other YouTube channels (titles, view counts) via an API key, used for content research.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to publish videos to the channel and to analyse their performance. Authorisation tokens and analytics data are stored locally on the channel owner's own computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. No data from other users is collected.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page. Locally stored tokens and analytics data are deleted when the application is removed.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the contact details on the channel's About page.
